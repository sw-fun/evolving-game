# Music credits

The castle's music is the user's own: composed by Michael A Wright with
music-pipe-rs (MIT), rendered from MIDI and encoded here as Ogg Opus
(`assets/music/`, made by `just music`). Some pieces quote a tune in the
public domain, in a phrase of their own; each quote is listed here with
its source, as music-pipe-rs's catalog gives it.

## Bridges

The bridges are short pieces that change the music fast, from one set
to another (`games/castle/music.toml`, `bridges` and `links`).

| Bridge | Plays | Quotes | Composer | Year | Source |
| ------ | ----- | ------ | -------- | ---- | ------ |
| to-victory | a battle won, to victory | See the Conquering Hero Comes (Judas Maccabaeus), its opening, in trumpets and horns | G. F. Handel | 1747 | music21 corpus: Aird's Airs book 3 (airdsAirs/book3.abc) |
| release | a battle (or the dungeon) left, to calm or explore | Passion Chorale "Wenn ich einmal soll scheiden" (St Matthew Passion), its first phrase in Bach's own harmony, in D minor, on strings and horn | J. S. Bach | 1727 | music21 corpus: bach/bwv244.62.mxl (SATB) |
| fall | a battle lost, to defeat (or into the dungeon) | Dies irae, its first phrase, in low horns and cellos over a held D | plainchant; quoted by Berlioz 1830 | 13th century | chant, written out by hand: F E F D E C D D |
