Evolving Game

  

    
A family of small castle-siege games that share one world and grow into one larger game, written in X_eTaL, the eXperimental Extensible Typed Array Language. This is its first, and smallest: a walk to the castle gate. (Evolving Game is a working title.)

    
What runs here. One X_eTaL program, the same file a terminal runs, compiled with its language to WebAssembly and running in this page. Twenty times a second the page tells it how much time has passed; a key or a click is told to it as it happens. It answers with a picture and a scene. The page draws them and decides nothing: no rule, number or word of the game is in the page. Sources shows the program and its libraries.

    
What draws it. The scene is drawn in 3D by the board player of X_eTaL-extensions, with Three.js. Where a browser cannot draw 3D, or with ?flat on the address, the program's own flat picture is shown. The wall, the gate, the bridge and the walker are models described in X_eTaL and made by it through Blender, their stone and wood baked from Blender's own textures.

    
With thanks to. Three.js 0.170.0 (MIT), kept with the page. Blender, which makes the models. JuliaMono (SIL Open Font License), the font of the sources, fetched when they are first opened.
